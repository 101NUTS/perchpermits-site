This website describes the tools and links to them; the tools themselves run on Apify. By using a Perch Permits tool you agree to these terms and to Apify's own terms for its platform and store.

3. What the data is, and what it isn't

The permits tool compiles government public records: Metro Nashville's open permit data and the Metro ePermits public case search. The floor-plan tool computes outputs from the specification you provide. In both cases:

  • Perch Permits republishes what the government publishes. Errors in the underlying public record will appear in the output. Missing values are returned as null, never guessed.
  • Outputs are information, not advice, and are provided "as is" without warranty of any kind. Floor plans and takeoffs are planning aids, not construction documents; verify dimensions on site before ordering materials.
  • No outcome is guaranteed. The tools identify public activity or compute a layout; they do not promise any commercial result.
